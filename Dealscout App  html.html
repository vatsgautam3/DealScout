📐 System Architecture & Design Gautam Vats

DealScout E-Commerce Price Intelligence & Basket Optimization Engine

1. Problem Statement

Online consumers spend 20+ minutes hopping between Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Blinkit, and BigBasket. Quick-commerce (Blinkit 10-min) offers convenience but often differs from warehouse e-commerce (Amazon) by 15-30%. No unified multi-store cart optimizer previously existed to split shopping lists across stores for maximal savings.

2. Core Architectural Pillars

  • Cross-Platform Price Normalizer: Standardizes variant specs, packaging sizes (e.g. 5L jars vs 1L pouches), bank discounts, coupon deductions, and delivery fees.
  • Optimal Cart Split Algorithm: Solves multi-store basket cost minimization using a greedy bipartite matching algorithm. Calculates single-store minimum vs multi-store delivery fee trade-offs.
  • Synthesized Web Audio Alerts: Avoids bulky audio asset downloads by synthesizing dual-oscillator frequencies (587.33 Hz → 1174.66 Hz) directly in browser memory.
  • Real-Time Currency Engine: Instant rate conversion across 6 global fiat currencies with localized symbol placement.
